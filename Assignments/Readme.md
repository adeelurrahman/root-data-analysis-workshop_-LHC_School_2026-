# Assignments

Submission includes the edited macro, the resulting plot, and a short explanation of the statistical choice you made.

## 1. Binning study

Use one Gaussian sample to compare several histogram binnings. Discuss resolution, bin fluctuations, and whether fitted parameters remain stable.

## 2. Model selection

The starter macro creates a Gaussian signal on top of a uniform background. A Gaussian-only fit is therefore incomplete. Build at least three candidate functions and compare reduced chi-square, and justify the preferred model
